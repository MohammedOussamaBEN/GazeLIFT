# Fifteen-seed gaze comparison and confidence intervals

Specified 26 October 2026 after the user's request for 10 or 15 runs and confidence measures. Fix 15 total initialization seeds, 40 through 54, before training seeds 45 through 54. Do not stop at ten or when a p-value becomes significant. Retain all previously completed A100 seeds 40 through 44. Compare the full GazeLIFT model against its no-gaze control on Kvasir-SEG and NCI-ISBI. This gives 60 fresh-initialized models: 20 audited prior runs plus 40 new runs. No H200 models are pooled into this matched-hardware study.

Preserve original architecture sources, saved splits, gaze data and bandwidths, preprocessing, optimizer, loss, augmentations, batch size, accumulation, native evaluation, threshold, maximum 80 epochs and validation patience 15. Only the training-seed assertion is changed in an isolated source copy. Frozen pretrained DINO cached features are retained; no learned segmentation checkpoint is reused. Checkpoints are selected by validation Dice, never test Dice. Two independent processes run per A100 with four torch threads each. A 50-minute scheduler wall limit avoids truncating slow runs; the epoch/early-stopping protocol does not change.

One replicate is the mean native test Dice from one independently trained model. Report mean and sample SD across 15 runs, retaining per-run case SD separately. For each dataset, pair full and no-gaze results by seed. Primary test remains two-sided exact paired sign-flip permutation, enumerating all 2^15=32768 assignments. Holm correction covers the two dataset-specific gaze comparisons at family-wise alpha 0.05. The family and test are not selected after seeing new results.

Confidence intervals: resample the 15 complete matched seed pairs with replacement 50000 times using a fixed analysis RNG seed 20261026. Reuse the same bootstrap index matrix for both methods and both datasets. Report percentile 95% intervals (2.5th and 97.5th percentiles) for each method mean and, primarily, the mean paired Dice gain. These are pointwise intervals for training-initialization variation conditional on the fixed test cohorts; test images/patients are not treated as extra training replicates. Separately retain paired t-tests and df=14 t intervals as assumption-dependent sensitivity analyses; do not substitute them according to which p is smaller.

All selected seeds, validation histories, source hashes, checkpoint hashes, exact test IDs and per-case metrics are retained. Each prediction mask is independently checked against its native ground truth. This is an extension of previously inspected experiments, not an untouched confirmatory cohort.
